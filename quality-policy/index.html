Quality Policy

Our commitment to reliable, secure, responsible and well-crafted work — and how we hold ourselves to it.

Our Commitment

Ticker Bell is committed to delivering reliable, secure, responsible and well-crafted products, research activities, educational services and professional solutions that meet applicable customer, contractual, legal and regulatory requirements.

We combine speed with disciplined execution. Quality, information security, privacy, learning effectiveness, innovation management and project governance are integrated into our work.

Scope

This policy applies, as relevant, to research and development; artificial intelligence and knowledge-based systems; software and digital product development; technology adaptation and transfer; innovation projects; consulting and mentorship; education and professional training; publishing and knowledge dissemination; collaborative research and industry partnerships; and project, programme and portfolio activities.

Our management principles

  • Understand customer, learner, partner and stakeholder needs.
  • Assign clear responsibilities and maintain accountability.
  • Manage important work through defined, connected processes.
  • Use evidence, testing, feedback and professional judgement.
  • Identify and treat quality, security, privacy and delivery risks.
  • Protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information.
  • Build privacy and responsible innovation into products and services.
  • Continuously review performance and implement corrective action.

Measurement and improvement

We establish measurable objectives appropriate to each activity, review the suitability and effectiveness of our management practices, learn from nonconformities and project retrospectives, and improve our products, services and organisational capability.

Standards alignment

This Quality Policy is aligned with the internationally recognised standards listed on our Standards & Compliance page.
