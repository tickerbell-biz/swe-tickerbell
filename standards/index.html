Standards & Compliance

The internationally recognised management-system standards our practices are built with reference to.

Management-System Framework

Ticker Bell intends to develop and continuously improve its management practices with reference to the following standards:

  • ISO 9001:2015 and Amendment 1:2024 — Quality management systems.
  • ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and Amendment 1:2024 — Information security management systems.
  • ISO/IEC 27701:2025 — Privacy information management systems.
  • ISO 21001:2025 — Management systems for educational organisations.
  • ISO 56001:2024 — Innovation management systems.
  • ISO 21502:2020 — Guidance on project management.
  • ISO 21505:2017 — Governance of projects, programmes and portfolios.

These standards provide complementary guidance for building an integrated approach to quality, security, privacy, education, innovation, project delivery and governance.

A note on certification

Reference to these standards does not, by itself, represent a claim of certification. Any certification status should be communicated separately and only after formal certification has been awarded by an accredited certification body.
