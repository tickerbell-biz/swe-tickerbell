Why we chose this journey

Why now is the right time to learn.

AI has changed how software is created. People no longer need to know everything before they begin, but they still need to understand how systems work, how to guide AI, and how to evaluate what it produces.

We started this journey to explore how a founder can use AI as a learning and development partner while gradually becoming an independent and responsible software builder.
