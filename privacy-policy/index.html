Privacy Policy

How Ticker Bell collects, uses, protects and retains personal information.

Last updated: January 27, 2025

1. Introduction

Ticker Bell ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and safeguard information when you visit our website or subscribe to our newsletter.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you subscribe, request information, or contact us. This may include your email address, name and communication data.

3. How we use information

  • Send newsletters, journal lessons and relevant updates.
  • Respond to enquiries and provide support.
  • Improve our website, content and services.
  • Notify you about changes to services or policies.

4. Data storage and service providers

We use Brevo as an email marketing and newsletter provider. Subscription information may be stored and processed by Brevo. We require service providers to maintain appropriate safeguards for personal information.

5. Data security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational security measures. No internet transmission or electronic-storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability of your personal data. You may unsubscribe through the link in our emails. Contact info@tickerbell.biz to exercise your rights.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described here or as required by law. Unsubscribing removes an address from active marketing, although limited records may be retained for compliance.

8. Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies. You can control them through browser settings, although disabling cookies may affect certain features.

9. International transfers

Information may be processed outside your country of residence. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

10. Children's privacy

Our services are not directed to people under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

11. Changes

We may update this policy and will publish the updated version and date on this page.

13. GDPR

For people in the European Economic Area, we process personal data lawfully, fairly and transparently. Processing is based on consent where you subscribe or contact us and, where applicable, our legitimate interests in providing relevant information and operating our services.
